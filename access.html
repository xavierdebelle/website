Every tool here works in your browser, no account needed. Sync — your data on every device, changes appearing live — is by invitation.

How it works

Ask once

Sign in with Google and your request goes to Xavier. Once he approves it, the Sync button in Project Phases, Budget Tracker and Idea Bank keeps your work in step between your phone and computer.

Your data is yours: each account only ever sees its own. Xavier runs the database, so as its administrator he could technically open it — he won’t, but you should know.
