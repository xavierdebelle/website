1 Sep 2026New homepage

The Map Became
A Point Cloud

The homepage mind map is no longer boxes joined by lines. It’s a single field of particles that forms the nodes and the links themselves, with the mark held sharp at the centre while everything around it drifts.

The layout is grown rather than placed — by space colonization, the algorithm behind tree branching and leaf venation — and then kept, so it doesn’t rearrange itself every time you open it.
