01 / Weddings

de Belle
Photography

Photographer & Cinematographer · The family studio

One of the originals. Thirty years of Montreal weddings and more than three thousand days on the other side of the lens.

I shoot and I film. The approach is documentary — I would rather be close enough that the real thing lands on camera than spend the day arranging people into a version of themselves. The finish is editorial: intentional, emotional, and never fussy about it.

Growing up inside a studio means you learn the unglamorous half early. How a timeline actually runs. What goes wrong at 4pm. Why the album matters more in ten years than the gallery does in ten days. Most of the tools on this site exist because of that half.

02 / Commercial

333
Photo

Commercial photography, video & design · @333mtl

The commercial lane. Photo, video, design, web and marketing from one team, for brands that would rather not manage five vendors.

Fashion, product, lifestyle, travel — the discipline changes, the standard doesn't. We work as an extension of your team instead of holding you at agency arm's length, and we'll tell you when the flashy idea is the wrong one.

It's a no-bull operation: transparent, collaborative, and built on treating a client's project with the same care we give our own. Proudly Montreal, proudly Canadian.

03 / Property

Real
Estate

Buying, renovating, holding · In progress

Buying a duplex, renovating it, and learning the trade the expensive way — by doing it.

Cameras teach you to see a space before it exists: what it becomes with the right light, the wall taken out, the clutter gone. Property is that same instinct on a much longer exposure. Find what nobody framed properly, put in the work, hold it.

This lane is the youngest of the three and the one I'm most deliberately growing. A duplex first, then whatever the duplex teaches me.
